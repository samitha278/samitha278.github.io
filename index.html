01 / About

Building efficient intelligence for machines that perceive and act.

My work connects multimodal learning, visual representation reuse, robot perception, and reinforcement learning. I am particularly interested in systems that reason effectively without unnecessary computation.

I am a final-year Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. I approach machine learning from first principles, combining research questions with careful implementation and empirical evaluation.

My long-term goal is to pursue a fully funded PhD and build an academic research career in efficient multimodal AI and robot intelligence.
