About

Who I Am

Researcher. Engineer. Builder.

I'm Samitha Sahan Bandara — an Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka (CGPA: 3.79/4.00, Dean's List Semesters 1–3), passionate about building AI systems that see, reason, and understand the world.

My research sits at the intersection of Computer Vision, Multimodal LLMs, and VLM Acceleration — specifically neuro-symbolic caching, spectral adapters, and efficient inference for edge deployment on resource-constrained hardware.

Currently a Visiting Research Student at Singapore Management University under Prof. Archan Misra, contributing to a NeurIPS 2026 paper on accelerated VLM inference.

Outside research I build foundational architectures from scratch (8+ GitHub projects), write technical essays on Medium, and contribute to open-source ML. I believe the best AI is built by those who care deeply about both the science and the humans it serves.
